Theory

Average output, Vm = peak phase voltage, continuous current:

1φ full-controlled: 2Vm·cosα/π · 1φ semi: Vm(1+cosα)/π

3φ full-controlled: 3√3·Vm·cosα/π · 3φ semi: 3√3·Vm(1+cosα)/2π

With α > 90° and a back-EMF or very inductive load, the converter inverts (mean Vo < 0). A freewheeling diode clamps Vo at zero and lets the load current decay without negative voltage.

Engine: ideal switches, exact RL(E) discretisation, 12 warm-up cycles, then one steady-state cycle at 720 points. Thyristors latch while current flows and commute instantly (no source inductance).
